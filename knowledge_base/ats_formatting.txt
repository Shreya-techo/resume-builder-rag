RESUME FORMATTING AND ATS COMPATIBILITY GUIDELINES

1. General Layout
Use a clean, professional, readable layout.
Prefer a simple single-column design for the initial resume template.
Use consistent spacing, alignment, margins, and typography.
Avoid decorative elements that make the document difficult to read.

2. Section Headings
Use clear, conventional headings when applicable:
- Professional Summary
- Skills
- Projects
- Education
- Experience
- Certifications

Include only sections relevant to the candidate.
Do not add empty sections or invent information to fill them.

3. Readability
Use concise bullet points rather than large paragraphs where appropriate.
Keep font sizes readable.
Maintain consistent date formats, bullet styles, and capitalization.
Avoid unnecessary symbols, images, complex tables, and text boxes in the initial ATS-friendly template.

4. Contact Information
Display only contact information supplied and approved by the candidate.
Do not invent a phone number, email address, location, or professional profile URL.
Respect any explicit exclusions.

5. Content Validation
Check spelling, grammar, sentence clarity, and consistency.
Verify that the resume does not contain unsupported achievements or qualifications.
Compare the final content with the original candidate information and approved changes.

6. Job-Relevance Checks
Check whether relevant, demonstrated skills and projects are represented.
Identify important job requirements that are not demonstrated.
Do not insert unsupported keywords merely to improve a match score.

7. Exclusion Rules
Check the candidate's excluded fields and preferences before approving the resume.
No explicitly excluded detail may appear in the final document.
If a required formatting check conflicts with a user preference, explain the issue and request a decision where necessary.

8. Critic Agent Output
Return a structured review containing:
- Pass or fail status
- Detected issues
- Severity of each issue
- Suggested corrections
- Whether another revision is required

9. Final Export
Export only approved content.
Check that text is not clipped, sections are not missing unexpectedly, and the document remains readable.
Do not claim that the resume is guaranteed to pass every ATS system. Different systems may parse documents differently.
